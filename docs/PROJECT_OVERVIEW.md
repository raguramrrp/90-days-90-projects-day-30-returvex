# RETURVEX — Project Overview

> **Tagline:** Turn return data into smarter business decisions.  
> **Domain:** E-Commerce Analytics / Business Intelligence / Machine Learning  
> **Platform Name:** RETURVEX  
> **Full Title:** E-Commerce Returns Intelligence & Profit Recovery Platform  

---

## 1. Executive Summary
In high-growth e-commerce retail, product returns represent one of the largest silent margin erosions. Across fashion, consumer electronics, and footwear, 15% to 30% of shipped orders are returned. Traditional reporting dashboards merely present gross refund sums, failing to address:
- **Net Financial Loss Disconnect**: Gross refunds are customer outflows, but reverse courier fees, restocking labor, product damage write-offs, and salvage recovery create the actual bottom-line impact.
- **Root Cause Opacity**: Merchants struggle to isolate whether returns stem from garment sizing discrepancies, vendor hardware defects, courier transit delays, or expectation mismatch.
- **Post-Hoc Reaction vs. Pre-Dispatch Prevention**: Interventions occur weeks after returns arrive at warehouses rather than flagging high-risk orders prior to dispatch.

**RETURVEX** solves these challenges by combining an enterprise analytics workstation, reproducible machine learning return risk classification, Pareto root cause investigation, and full financial ledger accounting into a single, cohesive local-first system.

## 2. Core Business Questions Answered
1. **Return Frequency**: What percentage of orders are being returned across rolling 7-day, 30-day, 90-day, and annual horizons?
2. **Product SKU Vulnerability**: Which specific catalog products have return rates significantly exceeding category averages?
3. **Category Profitability**: Which merchandise sectors incur the highest reverse logistics freight and scrap write-offs?
4. **Customer Drivers**: What are the primary reported reasons for return, and which vital few account for 80% of volume?
5. **Geographic Distribution**: Which regional corridors and states (e.g., remote Northeast hubs) exhibit courier delivery rejections?
6. **Financial Recovery**: How much cash is refunded to customers compared to direct operational logistics expenditure?
7. **Reverse Logistics Burden**: What is the financial burden of reverse courier transportation?
8. **Predictive Risk**: What is the statistical return probability of an order at the moment of checkout?
9. **Evidence & Quality Audits**: What verifiable evidence isolates supplier component defects vs. consumer sizing mismatch?
10. **Targeted Interventions**: Which targeted actions (WhatsApp size verification, carrier reassignment, prepaid incentives) mitigate return loss?
